Then — and this part matters

  1. Install the app and open it once.
  2. Go back to the message and tap the same link again.
  3. Copia will ask whether you want to join the list. Nothing is added until you say yes.

We can't carry the invitation across the install for you — iOS doesn't give apps a way to know which link brought you to the App Store. Tapping the original link again is the whole workaround, and it takes a second.

What Copia is

  • A shopping list that sorts itself into aisles and lays out the walk, so you cross the shop once.
  • Photograph a recipe page, or paste a link, and the ingredients land on the list with their measures.
  • Share a list with the people you live with. Everyone sees the same list, updating as it's ticked off.
  • Works offline. Your lists live on your phone; an account only syncs them between devices.
